01 / Problems I solve

Technology should remove friction, not create more of it.

You do not need to arrive with a technical specification. Start with the part of the business that is slow, repetitive or hard to trust.

01

“Our team keeps doing the same task by hand.”

Automate the repeatable work. Reduce errors and give people time back for higher-value decisions.

02

“Our systems do not talk to each other.”

Connect the flow. Move information between tools reliably, with logs and clear exception handling.

03

“We have data, but no clear picture.”

Turn data into decisions. Organize the signal and show what the team needs to act on next.

04

“We need a custom system—not another subscription.”

Build around the real workflow. Create software that fits the operation instead of forcing it into a generic product.
